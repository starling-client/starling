# Licensing FAQ

Sunrise's code is under the licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE): the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0 with the Sunrise
Additional Terms 1.0 above it. This page explains it in plain words; it isn't part of the licence, and where they
differ, the licence decides.

## What it covers

Everything in Sunrise up to commit `de8b4e2526` came from Horizon and ari's fork and is MIT (third-party parts keep
their own licences, see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)): anyone may take it, for any purpose.
Sunrise's licence covers what was added after that, except code imported from other projects, which
[LICENSES/IMPORTS](LICENSES/IMPORTS) lists with its own licence.

## Always fine, paid or not

- **Playing**, and everything a player does with Sunrise.
- **Servers**: running, hosting or renting them out, changed for your own operation or not, and selling things on
  them.
- **Mods**, plugins, resource packs, data packs, shaders and tools, under any licence, including paid ones. Whether
  you may sell a mod is up to Mojang's EULA and usage guidelines, which Sunrise's licence neither adds to nor
  overrides.
- **Videos, streams, guides, reviews and links** about Sunrise or a fork.
- **Stores, code hosts and mirrors** carrying unchanged copies, as long as they don't charge for the copies.
- **Dedicated server software**, changed or not, handed to others for free with its source and its own identity.
  Selling it, as a binary or otherwise, isn't allowed; charging to run or host a server is.

## Free forks and ports

Forks and ports are fine without asking, including ones for other platforms or devices (a mobile or handheld port,
a fork tuned for older hardware), as long as:

- **The source is public**, free and without sign-up, from the day of each release, under these same terms.
- **It's free**: the fork, its updates and its features cost nothing, downloads aren't behind ads, sign-ups or
  payment, and nothing in the fork, its launcher or installer, its built-in single-player server or the page you
  offer it from advertises, sells or unlocks anything paid. No ads, sponsor placements, shop, paid cosmetics,
  pay-to-win, tiers or early access. Outside servers and mods the player added themselves aren't part of the fork,
  whatever they sell.
- **Donations are welcome** through a plain link or button that never interrupts (no pop-ups, timers or nag
  screens) and unlocks nothing. Where the money goes is up to you.
- **Platforms are fine**: putting a fork on Modrinth, CurseForge, itch, a store or a code host is fine. The
  platform's own ads and sign-in don't count against you, as long as you take no share of that ad money.
- **It's marked unofficial**: say it's based on Sunrise and link to it. A name containing "Sunrise" is fine with
  "unofficial" beside it. Don't use Sunrise's logo or its sign-in, update, telemetry or signing services.

[LICENSE](LICENSE) has the exact conditions. Sunrise itself follows the same rules.

## What isn't allowed

Taking Sunrise's code into a product that competes with it and is closed, sold, ad-supported or monetized in any
of the ways above, including putting it into a monetized client or launcher. This holds whatever licence that
product uses.

## What else to know

- **Contributing:** there is no CLA. Contributors keep their copyright, their code comes in under the same licence
  it goes out under, and commits are signed off under the Developer Certificate of Origin
  ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-and-dco)). Nobody, Sunrise included, can put your code under other terms.
  Every author stays free to license their own contributions elsewhere too.
- **Later versions:** the project may publish later versions of the Additional Terms, which can only make the
  wording clearer or add permissions, and can never allow a closed or monetized competing product or give
  anyone special rights; you may use any later version.
- **When you share Sunrise**, pass on the licence (or its URL) and the `Required Notice:` line at the top of
  [LICENSE](LICENSE).
