# Q-001: may a `layer.*` rule move a full opaque cube?

- **Spec section:** revision 1, 4.2 (lines 588-593), "Render layers": `layer.solid`, `layer.cutout`, E
  `layer.cutout_mipped` and `layer.translucent` move blocks to another terrain layer; "Full opaque cubes cannot move,
  because culling, AO and light would change" `[OF-SH]`.
- **Question:** is that sentence a rule a pack can rely on in every tier, so that a `layer.*` rule naming a full opaque
  cube (an oak log, stone) is ignored for it, or does it describe one source only, with E-tier packs expecting such a
  cube to move? If it can move, what happens to the faces of its neighbours that the cube hides, which would show
  through a blended cube?
- **What was observed:** the request this was implemented for (sunrise-org/sunrise#341) gives as its control
  `layer.translucent=minecraft:oak_log:axis=x`, expecting the axis=x log to move to the translucent terrain and the
  axis=y log to stay. An oak log is a full opaque cube. Of the five local packs, Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3 writes
  `layer.translucent=glass glass_pane beacon` and Bliss 2.1.2 `layer.translucent = minecraft:glass minecraft:glass_pane`
  (under `#ifdef FORCE_TRANSLUCENT_GLASS`); none of those blocks is a full opaque cube. Sunrise follows 4.2 as written
  for now: a full opaque cube keeps its layer, and the pack's load log names the rule and the states.
